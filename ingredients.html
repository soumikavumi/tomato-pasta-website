Ingredients

A simple list you can adapt based on what you have at home.

  • 200g pasta (penne, fusilli, or spaghetti)
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (or 1–2 regular tomatoes, chopped)
  • 1 cup tomato passata or crushed tomatoes
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: basil/oregano, chili flakes, grated cheese

Quick tip

Add a splash of pasta water to the sauce to help it coat the pasta smoothly.

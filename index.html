A simple, satisfying weeknight recipe

This recipe combines a rich tomato base with fresh vegetables for a balanced, everyday pasta dish. It’s easy to follow, flexible (use the vegetables you have), and designed to stay clean and readable for beginners learning web development.

Tip

For extra flavor, simmer the tomato sauce for 10–15 minutes and finish with a small drizzle of olive oil.

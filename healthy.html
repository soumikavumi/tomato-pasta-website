Why this recipe is healthy

Tomato pasta with vegetables is a balanced meal that combines carbohydrates for energy with fiber, vitamins, and minerals from vegetables.

  • Vegetables add fiber: helps with fullness and digestion.
  • Tomatoes provide antioxidants: including lycopene and vitamin C.
  • Flexible nutrition: you can add more vegetables or legumes.
  • Controlled ingredients: homemade sauce can reduce added sugar and excess salt.
  • Healthy fats: olive oil adds flavor and supports nutrient absorption.

Simple upgrade

Use whole-wheat pasta or add chickpeas for extra fiber and protein.

Vitamins (per 100g cooked pasta)

These values are a simple example overview. Actual amounts vary by pasta type and brand.

Vitamin Approx. amount
Vitamin B1 (Thiamin) ~0.02 mg
Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin) ~0.02 mg
Vitamin B3 (Niacin) ~0.40 mg
Vitamin B9 (Folate) ~7 µg

Reminder

The vegetables and tomato sauce add additional vitamins (like vitamin C and A) beyond pasta alone.

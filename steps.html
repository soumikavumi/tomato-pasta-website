Cooking Steps

Follow these steps in order. Use the button to show/hide them.

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook pasta until al dente.
  2. While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add onion and cook for 2–3 minutes until softened.
  4. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds (do not burn it).
  5. Add bell pepper and zucchini. Cook for 4–6 minutes until tender.
  6. Add cherry tomatoes and tomato passata/crushed tomatoes.
  7. Season with salt, pepper, and optional herbs. Simmer 8–12 minutes.
  8. Reserve a little pasta water, then drain pasta.
  9. Toss pasta into the sauce. Add a splash of pasta water if needed.
  10. Serve warm. Optional: basil, chili flakes, or a light topping of cheese.

Timing

Simmering the sauce a bit longer helps deepen the tomato flavor.
